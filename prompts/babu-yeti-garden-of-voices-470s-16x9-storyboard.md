# Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices 470s Storyboard

High in a Himalayan rhododendron forest, little Babu Yeti grows proud of his sweet singing and boasts that no one else in the glade can sing. The forest friends quarrel with him until Mama Yeti comes and asks him which garden he likes best, one with only one kind of flower or one with many. He understands, says sorry, and the whole glade sings together at sunset. Gentle and warm with comic touches, then joyful. The moral: many voices together make the sweetest song of all.

This is the audio-first film of the story (section 9 of the `story-to-storyboard` skill). It is cut to the Google AI Studio recording of the whole story with the Flow Music song laid in, `story-with-song.wav` (7:44.5), so it is 47 videos of 10 seconds, horizontal 16:9, the last trimmed to 4.5 seconds in the edit. Every spoken word and its time comes from the recording, through the film timeline; each clip is generated in Flow with that speech so the mouths move to it, and the recording replaces Flow's audio when the film is assembled. Videos 35 to 45 carry `Song:` lines timed to the recorded song "Many Voices, One Song" so the on-screen singers move their mouths in time while non-singers keep their mouths closed, and the master recording still replaces Flow's audio in the edit. The saved Characters @Babu Yeti and @Mama Yeti carry their looks; so do the story cast @Gombu Tortoise, @Mili Red Panda, @Nimbu Tree Frog, @Meadow Mouse, and @Sona Butterfly, whose files are in `prompts/characters/`. After the final video come the two cover prompts.

## Story adaptation

The story file is the script, word for word; nothing is recast or rewritten here. The film has one location, the rhododendron glade.
