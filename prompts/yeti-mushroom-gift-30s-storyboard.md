# Yeti Mushroom Gift 30s Storyboard

A young village girl in a misty, moss-covered forest shares her last mushroom and a gourd of water with a spotted deer, then clears a patch of earth to forage and finds nothing, her basket empty. Babu Yeti has been peeking from behind the arched trunk and fetches Mama Yeti and Papa Yeti; Mama Yeti kneels at the bare earth, the light beneath her paw turns gold, and a dense cluster of mushrooms pushes up from the soil. The girl harvests with delight, Papa Yeti and Babu Yeti join in, Babu feeds the deer, and the film ends with the girl's basket overflowing and one mushroom given back to Babu. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines.
