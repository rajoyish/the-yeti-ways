# Yeti Plateau Cup 300s Storyboard

On a high Himalayan plateau, Papa Yeti, Mama Yeti, and Babu Yeti, with Snow Leopard in goal, play a football match against Wild Yak, Red Panda, and Alpine Musk Deer, with Polar Bear in goal, in front of a packed crowd of mountain animals. Team B goes ahead twice. At half-time Babu Yeti is sad about an open chance he missed, and Mama Yeti comforts him. In the second half Mama Yeti and Papa Yeti each score, Snow Leopard makes a late save, and Babu Yeti scores the 3-2 winner. The point of the film is that courage comes back when your family believes in you, and that both teams play fair. It is written for AI video generation following the character rules, the environment lock, the cinematic direction, and the social media prompt guidelines. There is no dialogue. Gesture, expression, and sound tell the whole story.

At the user's request this film is 30 videos of 10 seconds each, a 5-minute runtime, in horizontal 16:9 widescreen. The house format is six vertical 9:16 videos. Wherever the house cards and prompts say vertical 9:16, this film says horizontal 16:9, and the operator sets Flow's orientation to landscape 16:9 before generating each clip. The climax, Babu Yeti's winning goal, comes in Video 26 rather than at the midpoint, because a match story peaks at its late winner.

Everything below is written for the Google Flow Agent: three instruction cards for the project, one setup prompt, and one Agent prompt per video. The Image map and the `Images:` lines say which images go in the prompt box for each paste. The saved Characters @Papa Yeti, @Mama Yeti, and @Babu Yeti carry the Yetis' looks, so nothing here describes them. The five animal players have no Character files yet, so each Agent prompt describes them in its `Cast:` line. Plateau Crowd and Monal Referee are always described only.
