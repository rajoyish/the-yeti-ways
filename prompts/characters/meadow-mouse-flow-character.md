# Meadow Mouse Flow Character

One of three identical tiny field mice of the glade, who drum their feet to the music, and the littlest of whom wants to ask Mama Yeti, built from the story cast of the Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-470s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 2, 4, 8, 9, 14, 17 to 19, 21, 22, 33, 38, 39, 44, 45, and 47. The storyboard calls the group `exactly three Meadow Mice, each @Meadow Mouse`: one Character, counted once. Linked in 4, 14, and 17, described-only in the others).

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's description is kept (tiny honey-brown field mice, big round pink-lined ears, cream bellies, black bead eyes, long thin tails, wearing nothing, each about the size of a pinecone). Gaps filled: a small pink nose with fine white whiskers, bright catchlights in the eyes, four tiny pink fingers on each front paw and five tiny pink toes on each back foot with no sharp claws, and the tail named pink-brown. The shot lines (they drum their feet, hop, wave their front paws, stand on their back feet, cross their paws, point) need no other feature. All three are drawn from this one design. Nothing added for originality.
