# Nimbu Tree Frog Flow Character

A chattery, excitable little tree frog who watches the quarrel from a branch of the old tree and calls down to suggest the Rhino Chief, built from the story cast of the Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-470s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 4, 15 to 17, 33, 39, and 47; linked as a Character in 4, 16, 17, 33, and 39, described-only in 47).

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (a small, bright lime-green tree frog, big round golden eyes, a cream throat, round orange toe pads, wearing nothing, about the size of an apple). Gaps filled: smooth, softly glossy skin, dark round pupils with bright catchlights, a wide friendly mouth, a throat that puffs out round when he calls (his shots show it), four thin fingers on each front foot and five long toes on each back foot, every digit ending in a round orange pad, and no tail. Nothing added for originality.
