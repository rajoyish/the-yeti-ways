# Sona Butterfly Flow Character

A large golden butterfly who flutters ahead through the trees and leads Mama Yeti into the glade, built from the story cast of the Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-470s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 19, 20, and 47; linked as a Character in 19 and 20, described-only in 47).

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (a large butterfly, wide amber-gold wings edged in black with small white spots, a thin dark body, about the size of a large leaf). Gaps filled: four wings with the same pattern on the upper and under sides, a soft velvety dark brown body, two thin dark antennae with rounded tips, six thin dark legs, and large glossy dark cartoon eyes with catchlights. A butterfly has no fingers or toes; each leg ends in one small tip. No tail. Nothing added for originality.
