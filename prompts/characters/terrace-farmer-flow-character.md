# Terrace Farmer Flow Character

A sturdy, kind-hearted hill farmer whose homestead sits at the foot of a steep Himalayan slope, built from the story cast of the Hillside Roots storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/papa-yeti-hillside-roots-30s-storyboard.md` (Videos 1 to 3)

Gaps filled: a broad, sturdy, rounded build, thick black eyebrows, a broad rounded nose, the tunic wrapped and tied at the left side and falling to mid-thigh with long sleeves rolled to the forearm, a small ear on each side, bare hands with five fingers each, and boots that reach the lower calf below the rolled trousers. Nothing added for originality; the storyboard's design is already its own.

## Character info

```text
Terrace Farmer is a sturdy, hard-working hill farmer who tends a small vegetable plot beside his wooden hut at the foot of a steep slope. He is warm, steady, and humble: he works with his whole body, hoeing, kneeling to dig, and patting soil down with his palms. When frightened he stumbles, looks back over his shoulder, and his eyes go wide; when amazed his mouth falls open; when hopeful or grateful he smiles warmly and presses his palms together at his chest in thanks. He moves with solid, grounded steps in his felt boots, and his only sounds are effort breaths, a startled gasp, and a soft, relieved laugh. He never speaks. He is awed by Papa Yeti, then trusting and thankful, never afraid of him; Papa Yeti stands well over a head taller than him. Look: broad and sturdy, warm brown skin, round friendly face, short black hair, thick black moustache, warm brown eyes, plain grey wrapped homespun tunic with rolled sleeves, faded rust-red sash, grey trousers rolled to the shin, black felt boots, five fingers on each bare hand. Never photoreal, scary, a different design, or with extra fingers.
```
