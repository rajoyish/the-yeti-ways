# Snowy Owl Flow Character

Babu Yeti's plump, gentle owl friend who carries the herb pouch through the night, built from the story cast of the Moonlit Herb Rescue storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-moonlit-herb-rescue-30s-storyboard.md` (Videos 1 to 3)

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Animals:` sentence is kept (plump, round body, soft white feathers flecked with tiny charcoal speckles, large round amber eyes, short hooked grey beak, fluffy feathered white feet, nothing on its legs). Gaps filled: a big round cartoon head with no ear tufts, broad rounded white wings with a few charcoal speckles, four short feathered toes on each foot ending in small, blunt, rounded charcoal-grey talon tips (the storyboard has it grip the pouch's red cord in its feet, so it needs toes that can hold), and a short, rounded white tail fan. The front pose spreads its wings partly open so the wing shape reads. Nothing added for originality.

## Character info

```text
Snowy Owl is Babu Yeti's loyal owl friend, a plump, gentle snowy owl who watches over the Yeti family at night and helps them by carrying small things through the air. It is calm, curious, and brave: it perches quietly on low branches beside Babu Yeti, tilts its round head to watch, and swoops down at once when he needs help. It flies with slow, soft, silent wingbeats, glides in to land with its wings spread wide, and grips light things, like a cord or a small cloth pouch, in its feathered feet. Its only sounds are soft hoots and the faint rustle of its wings. It is fond of all three Yetis and settles near them, never fearful of them. Look: plump, round, egg-shaped body, big round head, soft white feathers flecked with tiny charcoal speckles, large round amber eyes, short hooked grey beak, no ear tufts, broad rounded white wings, four short feathered toes on each foot with small, blunt, charcoal-grey talon tips, a short rounded white tail fan, and nothing on its legs. Never photoreal, fierce, a different owl design, or with long sharp talons.
```
