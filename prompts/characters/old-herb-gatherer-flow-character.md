# Old Herb-Gatherer Flow Character

A kind old grandfather who carries healing herbs home through the snowy night by lantern light, built from the story cast of the Moonlit Herb Rescue storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-moonlit-herb-rescue-30s-storyboard.md` (Videos 1 and 3)

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Humans:` sentence is kept (round rosy nose, bushy white eyebrows, kind deep-set brown eyes, short frosty white beard, knee-length padded brown wool coat, moss-green cloth sash, rounded grey felt cap with ear flaps, grey felt boots, woven-reed lantern with a glowing candle). Gaps filled: warm light-tan skin, a stocky, slightly stooped build, dark brown wool trousers tucked into the boots, bare hands with five fingers each, the lantern always in his right hand (as in Video 1), and ears covered by the cap's flaps. The herb pouch is left out: it is a story prop that passes to the owl, not part of him. Nothing added for originality; the storyboard's design is already its own.

## Character info

```text
Old Herb-Gatherer is a kind, elderly grandfather who gathers healing herbs and carries them home through the snowy mountain night. He is gentle, devoted, and a little frail: he hurries when worried, clutches what matters to his chest, glances back over his shoulder when uneasy, and sits slumped with empty open hands when sad. When hope returns he gasps, his eyes widen, and his rosy cheeks lift into a warm smile. He moves with short, careful steps in his felt boots, the lantern swaying from his right hand, and makes only soft sounds: crunching footsteps, a sharp breath, a quiet sigh. He never speaks. He trusts the Yetis and their owl friend and is grateful, never afraid of them. Look: stocky, slightly stooped, warm light-tan skin, round rosy nose, kind deep-set brown eyes, bushy white eyebrows, short frosty white beard, rounded grey felt cap with ear flaps, knee-length padded brown wool coat with a moss-green sash, dark brown trousers, grey felt boots, five fingers on each bare hand, and a woven-reed lantern with a glowing candle in his right hand.
```
