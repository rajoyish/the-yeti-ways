# Mili Red Panda Flow Character

A gentle, calm red panda who agrees with Gombu Tortoise that Babu Yeti should not be so full of himself, and later sings with him, built from the story cast of the Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-470s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 2, 3, 7 to 9, 12 to 14, 18, 19, 21, 22, 29, 32, 35, 36, 38, 41, 42, 44 to 47; linked in 18 of them and described-only in 19, 22, 41, 45, and 47).

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (a small red panda, russet-red fur, a cream-white face with russet marks under the eyes, round cream-edged ears, dark brown legs, a long bushy tail ringed in russet and pale bands, bright dark eyes, wearing nothing, about the size of a small dog). Gaps filled: a dark brown chest and belly matching the legs, as a red panda has, a white muzzle and a small black nose, dark pupils with bright catchlights, five short dark toes on each of her four paws with no sharp claws, and her ringed tail's pale bands named pale cream. The shot lines (she whistles through pursed lips, twitches and curls her tail, puffs her cheeks and huffs, sits up and stands on her back legs) need no other feature. Nothing added for originality.
