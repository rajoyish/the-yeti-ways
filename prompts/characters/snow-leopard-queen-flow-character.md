# Snow Leopard Queen Flow Character

A calm, regal snow leopard who rules the valley from her hilltop palace, hears the whole truth from Papa Yeti, and forgives him. Built from the story cast of the Two Coins of Wisdom storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 14 to 16; the same cast as `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-storyboard.md`). Linked as a Character in all three videos.

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (regal, thick pale smoky-grey fur dotted with dark rosettes, pale green eyes, a small pink nose, a long thick ringed tail, a slim golden circlet, a deep-blue woven sash with gold edging and no writing). Gaps filled:
- A soft cream-white chest and belly, as a snow leopard has.
- Small rounded ears with dark backs, and fine white whiskers.
- Dark pupils and bright catchlights in the pale green eyes, and calm, gentle brows.
- Large, soft, furry paws, each with four rounded toes, claws hidden.
- The circlet: one plain, smooth, slim gold band, no gems or symbols.
- The sash: worn over one shoulder and across the body, deep blue with a thin gold edge, no writing.
- A calm, dignified, gentle closed-mouth smile at rest; her stern moments are acting, not design.

She sits on her throne in every shot, so she is drawn sitting tall on her haunches. Her size stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line.
