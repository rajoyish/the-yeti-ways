# Dolma Yak Flow Character

A kind, slow, gentle old yak who keeps a little stone tea stall at a high mountain pass and gives Papa Yeti his second, truest piece of wisdom. Built from the story cast of the Two Coins of Wisdom storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Video 7; the same cast as `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-storyboard.md`). Linked as a Character in Video 7.

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (a gentle old yak with no horns, long shaggy chocolate-brown hair, a greying cream muzzle, kind half-closed brown eyes, a soft woven red shawl over her shoulders). Gaps filled:
- Small, soft, drooping ears half hidden in her hair, and a broad, rounded dark grey nose.
- A gentle closed-mouth smile at rest.
- Four sturdy legs, each ending in a dark grey cloven hoof split into two rounded toes.
- A long tail of shaggy chocolate-brown hair ending in a soft tuft.
- The shawl's pattern: plain woven red wool with one thin cream stripe along each edge and a short fringe, no symbols or writing.

Hornless on purpose: a horned animal standing beside a Yeti makes video models add horns to the Yeti. She stands on four legs behind her counter in the film, so she is drawn standing on all four. Her size stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line.
