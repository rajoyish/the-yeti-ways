# Woolly Hare Flow Character

A small, round, shy woolly hare, one of three identical hungry neighbours who cannot reach the mangoes and later share the feast. Built from the story cast of the Sour Mango Trick storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-sour-mango-trick-150s-storyboard.md` (Videos 2, 12, 13, and 15). The storyboard calls the group `exactly three Woolly Hares, each @Woolly Hare`: one Character, counted once.

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (small and round, soft grey-brown fur, long upright ears with dark tips, big brown eyes, fluffy white tails). Gaps filled:
- Pale pink inner ears and a small pink nose.
- Fine white whiskers.
- A small closed mouth.
- Bright catchlights in the eyes.
- Short front legs and long, padded back feet, with four small furry toes on each foot and no visible claws.
- An oversized cartoon head.

Their size (about a loaf of bread) stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line. They do not speak. Nothing was added for originality.

## Character info

```text
Woolly Hare is a small, round woolly hare who lives at the edge of a forest clearing, always seen in a group of three identical hares. It is shy, gentle, and hopeful, quick to hide in tall ferns and quick to peek out again when it feels safe. It hops in short, springy bounds, sits up on its long back feet, twitches its long ears at sounds, and nibbles fruit held in its front paws. Its only sounds are soft little squeaks, sniffs, and the patter of its feet; it never speaks. Around the Yetis it is trusting and happy, and it sits in a row with the other hares to share food. It is about the size of a loaf of bread. Look: thick grey-brown fur, long upright ears with dark brown tips and pink insides, big glossy brown eyes, a pink nose with white whiskers, short front legs and long back feet with four furry toes on each foot and no visible claws, a fluffy round white tail, and nothing worn.
```
