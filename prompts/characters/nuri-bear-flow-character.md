# Nuri Bear Flow Character

A baby polar bear cub, converted from a realistic wildlife-photo draft into a stylized 3D CGI character that can stand in a scene with the Yetis.

Draft changes: dropped the scene and the photo terms ("realistic eye-level wildlife photograph", the flat snowy ridge, the blurred snowy Arctic hills, "diffused natural daylight", "shallow depth of field") and the turn to the right (the front view faces straight on; the turnaround shows the turns). Kept the fluffy white fur, the upright stance on the hind legs, the front paws raised near the chest, and the black paw pads, which the front view shows by turning the raised paws palm-out toward the camera. Filled the gaps: warm creamy-white fur (so she never reads as a white Yeti, the round ears, black nose, and bear muzzle do the rest), large dark-brown eyes, a black button nose and a small black-lined smiling mouth, five short rounded toes on every paw with black pads and no visible claws, and a tiny round stubby tail. Added one original detail: pale pink inner ears. The name and "she" are suggestions; rename it before you save the Character. The Arctic ridge belongs in a storyboard's `Environment:` sentence, not in the Character.

## Character info

```text
Nuri Bear is a baby polar bear cub, gentle, curious, and a little shy, and a friend the Yetis meet in the snow. She is playful once she trusts someone, easily startled, and proud of small wins. She usually walks on all four paws with a rolling, bouncy waddle, rises onto her hind legs to look around or reach for something, holds her front paws up near her chest when she is unsure, and rolls onto her back or bats at snowflakes when she is happy. She huffs, grunts softly, and gives small squeaky bleats, and never speaks words. Around the Yetis she stands on her hind legs about as tall as Babu Yeti's shoulders; she shadows Babu Yeti, nuzzles a Yeti's leg for comfort, and copies what the grown-ups do. Look: solid creamy-white fur, small round ears with pale pink insides, a short muzzle, a black button nose, a thin black lip line, large dark-brown cartoon eyes, five rounded toes with black pads on every paw and no visible claws, a tiny stubby tail, nothing worn. Always the same stylized 3D character, never photoreal, never scary, never a Yeti, never another bear design.
```

3. Edit only her head. Keep everything else exactly as it is. Give her two small round bear ears with pale pink inner ears, a short rounded muzzle, and a black button nose, and remove any tuft of fur on top of her head, so she reads clearly as a polar bear cub and not a Yeti.
