# Grand Peacock Flow Character

A haughty, pompous peacock, one of three identical important-looking peacocks who pay Papa Yeti to bury a "smelly old bundle". Built from the story cast of the Two Coins of Wisdom storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 8 to 10; the same cast as `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-storyboard.md`). The storyboard calls the group `exactly three Grand Peacocks, each @Grand Peacock`: one Character, counted once. Linked as a Character in all three videos.

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (haughty, shimmering deep-blue neck and breast, a small fan-shaped crest, a long folded green-and-bronze train of eye-spotted feathers, proud half-lidded eyes, wearing nothing, all three identical). Gaps filled:
- Dark brown eyes with bright catchlights, under heavy, proud half-closed lids.
- A small, short ivory-grey beak.
- Wings of deep blue at the shoulders fading to soft bronze-brown at the tips. They fan the air, point, and tip coins in the shots.
- Two slender grey legs, each foot with four toes, three forward and one back, blunt and rounded.
- The train stays folded, never fanned open, as the storyboard has it.
- A lofty, nose-in-the-air closed-beak look at rest, pompous rather than mean.

His height (about the handcart's wheel) stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line.
