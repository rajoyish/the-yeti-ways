# Blue Magpie Flow Character

A sly, showy magpie who keeps a little trinket stall on a pine-forest road and sells Papa Yeti his first piece of wisdom, meaning it as a trick. Built from the story cast of the Two Coins of Wisdom storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 5 and 6; the same cast as `prompts/papa-yeti-two-coins-of-wisdom-180s-storyboard.md`). Linked as a Character in both videos.

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (sleek, glossy black head and chest, a white patch on the crown, bright blue back and wings, coral-red beak and feet, sly amber eyes, a very long blue tail with white-tipped feathers, wearing nothing). Gaps filled:
- A soft pale grey belly below the black chest.
- Dark pupils and bright catchlights in the amber eyes.
- Coral-red feet with four slender toes each, three pointing forward and one back, with small blunt, rounded toe tips.
- Wing feathers that can lift and spread like a hand, because he raises one wing like a teacher in Video 6; he plucks the coin with his beak, not his feet.
- A bright, cheeky closed-beak look at rest, sly rather than mean.

His size (about a pigeon, with a long tail) stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line. Nothing was added for originality beyond these choices.
