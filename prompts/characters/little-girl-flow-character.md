# Little Girl Flow Character

Old Herb-Gatherer's little granddaughter, a sweet five-year-old in a cream nightgown, built from the story cast of the Moonlit Herb Rescue storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-moonlit-herb-rescue-30s-storyboard.md` (Video 3)

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Humans:` sentence is kept (a child of about five, short dark hair in two tiny tufts, round cheeks, soft cream nightgown). Gaps filled: warm light-tan skin to match her grandfather, large warm brown eyes like her mother's, dark brown hair, a small button nose, an ankle-length long-sleeved nightgown with no pattern, bare feet with five toes each, and five fingers on each hand. Rule fix: the storyboard shows her "pale, sleepy" in Video 3; the Character is drawn healthy, rosy, and smiling, as a child is never shown ill in Character images, and the scene's acting supplies the sleepiness. Nothing added for originality.

```text
Little Girl is Old Herb-Gatherer's granddaughter, a sweet, gentle child of about five. She is quiet, trusting, and affectionate: she nestles into her mother's arms, peeks out from under a quilt, and, when she feels better, opens her eyes wide and beams with a bright, healthy smile. She moves softly and a little sleepily, reaches out with small open hands, and her only sounds are soft breaths, a tiny yawn, or a small happy giggle. She never speaks. She is always with a grown-up who looks after her, never alone and never in danger. She is curious and delighted, never frightened, when she glimpses a Yeti or the owl. Look: a small five-year-old with warm light-tan skin, full round rosy cheeks, a small button nose, large warm brown eyes, short dark brown hair in two tiny tufts on top of her head, a plain cream ankle-length long-sleeved nightgown, bare feet with five toes each, and five fingers on each hand.
```
