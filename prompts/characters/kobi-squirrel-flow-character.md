# Kobi Squirrel Flow Character

A plump, proud, chattery giant flying squirrel who hoards the mangoes of an old forest tree, then learns to share. Built from the story cast of the Sour Mango Trick storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-sour-mango-trick-150s-storyboard.md` (Videos 1, 2, 4 to 11, 14, and 15)

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description and cover sentence is kept: plump and lively; rich chestnut-red fur; a cream tummy and chest; big round glossy dark eyes with bright catchlights; small rounded ears; a small pink nose with fine whiskers; furry gliding flaps between each front and back leg; small dark paws with tiny fingers; a long, fluffy chestnut-red tail; nothing worn. Gaps filled from the shot lines, where he hugs a mango in both paws, wags one tiny finger, scratches his head, folds his arms, and stamps his feet:
- His hands: four small, separate dark fingers on each front paw.
- His feet: five small dark toes on each back foot.
- Tiny rounded fingertips, with no sharp claws.
- Dark brown irises.
- A small closed mouth, with two small, blunt front teeth showing only when he talks.
- Dark whiskers.
- A cartoon-sized head, rather larger than a real squirrel's.

His size (about a house cat) stays in the storyboard's `Cast:` line, because a Character image cannot carry scale. His design is already the story's own, so nothing was added for originality.
