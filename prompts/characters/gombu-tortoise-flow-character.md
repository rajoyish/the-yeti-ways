# Gombu Tortoise Flow Character

A big, old, slow, kind tortoise who hums low and deep, gently tells Babu Yeti that it is better when others praise you, and forgives him, built from the story cast of the Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices storyboard in the stylized 3D style of the Yeti films.

Source: `prompts/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-470s-16x9-storyboard.md` (Videos 2, 3, 7 to 11, 13, 15, 18, 19, 21, 22, 29 to 32, 34 to 38, 41, 43 to 47; linked as a Character in all of them).

Draft changes: every trait from the storyboard's `Cast:` description is kept (big, old, gentle tortoise, high-domed moss-green shell patterned in soft olive plates, sand-brown scaly head and four stumpy legs, kind droopy-lidded amber eyes, a wide, slow smile, wearing nothing, about the size of a big round boulder). Gaps filled: a pale cream-yellow underside shell, dark pupils with bright catchlights, a small rounded toothless mouth, five short, blunt, rounded toes on each of his four feet with no sharp claws, and a tiny short tail tucked under the back edge of the shell, as a tortoise has. The shot lines (he hums with his mouth closed, blinks slowly, lifts his head, shakes it, speaks) need no other feature. Nothing added for originality; the storyboard's design is already its own.
