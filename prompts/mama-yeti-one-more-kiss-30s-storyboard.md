# Mama Yeti One More Kiss 30s Storyboard

On a snowy night in the family cottage, Papa Yeti and Mama Yeti sit side by side on the olive-green sofa by the hearth. Papa Yeti gives her one sleepy kiss on the cheek and dozes off, but for Mama Yeti one kiss is never enough: she kisses him back, waits, kisses him again, and when he only smiles in his sleep she folds her arms in a big pink-cheeked pout, watched from the hearth by Pebble Hedgehog. Papa Yeti wakes, sees the pout, and wins her back with a hug and a flurry of little kisses, while Tofu Cat blinks contentedly from the windowsill and Babu Yeti pads in from the hall to join the cuddle. The tone runs from cosy and playful to sulky, then to warm and tender. It is cut shot by shot, one subject at a time, and has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines.

The video workflow below is written for the Google Flow Agent: three instruction cards for the project, one setup prompt, and one Agent prompt per video. Separate horizontal and vertical cover prompts follow the final video. The Image map and the `Images:` lines say which images go in the prompt box for each paste. The saved Characters @Papa Yeti, @Mama Yeti, and @Babu Yeti carry every Yeti's look, and @Pebble Hedgehog and @Tofu Cat are described in each prompt until their Characters are saved, so the video prompts do not describe the Yetis. The cover prompts carry their full character locks for an independent image model.
