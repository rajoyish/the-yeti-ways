# Humpty Dumpty — 220s Storyboard (16:9)

Babu Yeti leads a bright springtime countdown in a Himalayan meadow. Ten living cartoon Humpty Dumpty eggs sit on a mossy stone wall, swinging their legs and waving. In each verse exactly one egg tumbles onto a soft moss cushion, and its shell cracks into two halves as one fluffy chick hatches out, which is why nobody can put Humpty together again. The king's horses (two living knitted toy ponies) and the king's men (two living felt toy guards) march out of a little stone toy castle every verse with a new, funnier plan to mend the shell, and the chick always hops free. Papa Yeti and Mama Yeti watch, laugh, and gather the growing flock of chicks. All 11 supplied stanzas remain unchanged, including “Ten Humpty Dumpty” and the final “Didn't”. Newly generated off-screen AI children's vocals, accompaniment, and video are generated together in Google Flow; visible characters act silently.

The film has 22 horizontal 16:9 clips of 10 seconds each, 720p, lasting 03:40. The saved Characters @Babu Yeti, @Papa Yeti, and @Mama Yeti carry the video designs. The file also holds 1 pick-up clip for the edit, after the numbered videos. The clip model and settings below follow this repository's existing configuration, rather than a fresh verification of the current Flow interface. Both promotional cover prompts are separate from the clip chain.

Each stanza has four lines. The first couplet pairs wall/fall, while men/again forms the closing rhyme; the same four melodic phrases repeat as only the initial count changes, with the final stanza also changing “One” to “No” and “Couldn't” to “Didn't”. Stress the count, Humpty Dumpty, wall/fall, horses/men, and together/again naturally. The line about the royal helpers is denser, so use light syllabic eighth notes and avoid a long sustain before “men”. These are proposed musical choices, not claims about the reference recording.

Each verse is one small visual cycle in two clips. Odd videos (the A half: count and fall): the row of eggs on the wall, then one tight Close Shot of only the rightmost egg as something playful (a wave, a butterfly, a sneeze, a dance, a stretch, a bee, a peek, a hop) tips him off, then a Yeti reaction. Even videos (the B half: the king's horses and men): the little castle door opens, the two ponies and two guards march out with a new plan (pushing, a daisy chain, a leaf, a pony clap, a shell lid, honey, yarn, push-and-pull, a rolling ball, everyone at once), the chick hops free, and the Yetis react. The last verse shows the empty wall, no fall, and a happy parade instead of a repair. No wooden toys, blocks, or other block-shaped props appear anywhere. The eggs, chicks, ponies, and guards are living, animated cartoon objects; none needs a saved Flow Character, because each prompt describes them in its Cast line. Nobody is hurt: every fall lands on soft moss, every shell opens cleanly into two smooth halves, and the chick inside is always cheerful.

### Count ledger and continuity review

**Why Flow showed two falls before, and the fix.** The old fall shot framed the whole row while one toy tipped, so Flow animated two. Now every fall happens in its own Close Shot that frames only the rightmost egg ("exactly one Humpty Dumpty egg ... the rest of the row is out of frame to the left and stays seated and still"), and the wide row shot before it has no fall in it. The count is stated in the wide row shot only, where nothing moves off the wall. If a take still shows two eggs falling or two chicks hatching, reject it.

The eggs sit from the castle (left) end, and the rightmost one falls each time, so the empty stretch of wall grows from the right. Each fallen shell's two halves are carried back into the castle by the guards (or left behind on the moss, out of later shots); they are never counted and never return to the wall. Chicks join one uncounted flock with Mama Yeti, so no shot asks Flow to count chicks.
