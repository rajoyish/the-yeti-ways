# Babu Yeti and the Starway Home — 60s Storyboard

Babu Yeti carries a fallen golden star across an ice bridge and a garden of luminous mushrooms, then rides a larger golden star through the clouds to return the little star to its constellation arch. Two newly invented companions help: Tavi Moon Moth shows the route, and Oru Cloud Ray opens a passage through the cloud bank. The tone is playful wonder, ending in a shared moment of gratitude beneath the restored constellation.

This 30-second expansion preserves the 20-second storyboard's progression from landing to running, bouncing, delight, star flight, cloud breakthrough, and a constellation finale. Babu Yeti is the only Yeti in the film; none of the source storyboard's characters are cast. His canonical identity comes from @Babu Yeti, without the source character's costume or design. The small star stays in Babu Yeti's left hand until its return; both stars are inanimate props.

The storyboard uses the project's Agent-prompt format. Babu returns the star at the 15-second midpoint; the remaining time lets the completed arch, relief, and companions' gratitude settle. Videos 4 to 6 add a 30-second Starway safari epilogue, which takes the film to 60 seconds in six 10-second videos. Babu Yeti rides the star glider out along the Starway with his two companions to watch a drifting herd of four Lumen Grazers. These videos are built around Extreme Wide Shots, overhead Bird's-Eye View aerials, and Tracking shots. In every wide and overhead shot Babu Yeti stays still and small, and he acts only in Full Shots or closer. The file includes three instruction cards, one setup prompt for two location plates, an Image map, six video prompts, and independent horizontal and vertical cover prompts. Character preparation files are available for [Tavi Moon Moth](characters/tavi-moon-moth-flow-character.md), [Oru Cloud Ray](characters/oru-cloud-ray-flow-character.md), and [Lumen Grazer](characters/lumen-grazer-flow-character.md). Their front and turnaround reference images are now generated under `references/characters/` and linked from the Character files. Upload those images and save the Characters in Flow before using the name-only video Cast lines. The cover prompts carry their complete text descriptions; this preparation has not created Flow Character assets.
