# Babu Yeti and the Starway Home — 20s Storyboard

Babu Yeti carries a fallen golden star across an ice bridge and a garden of luminous mushrooms, then rides a larger golden star through the clouds to return the little star to its constellation arch. Two newly invented companions help: Tavi Moon Moth shows the route, and Oru Cloud Ray opens a passage through the cloud bank. The tone is playful wonder, ending in quiet satisfaction.

This adaptation keeps the source's 20-second runtime and its progression from landing to running, bouncing, delight, star flight, cloud breakthrough, and a constellation finale. Babu Yeti is the only Yeti in the film; none of the source storyboard's characters are cast. His canonical identity comes from @Babu Yeti, without the source character's costume or design. The small star stays in Babu Yeti's left hand until its return; both stars are inanimate props.

The storyboard uses the project's Agent-prompt format, adapted to two 10-second videos. It includes three instruction cards, one location setup prompt, an Image map, two video prompts, and independent horizontal and vertical cover prompts. Tavi Moon Moth and Oru Cloud Ray have no Character files yet and are described in their video Cast lines. Their new designs are repeated in full in the covers.
