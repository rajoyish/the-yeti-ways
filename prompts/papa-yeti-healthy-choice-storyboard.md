# Papa Yeti Healthy Choice Storyboard

This storyboard portrays a lighthearted interaction where Papa Yeti chooses a bright red apple over the burgers and fries Mama Yeti offers him, and Mama Yeti ends up joining him. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines.

Production note: the story runs 30 seconds as three separate 10-second videos. Generate each video on its own, then cut them together in order. Every shot repeats the same environment, prop, and character text so the clips match. Use the final frame of the previous video as the image reference when generating the next one.

Every video's table runs on its own 00:00 - 00:10 clock. Paste one table into Flow at a time, and paste only the table. The `Film position:` line under each heading is where the clip sits in the finished 30-second cut; it is for the editor, not for Flow. If Flow produces more than one clip, a clip longer than 10 seconds, or a timing error, the prompt contained timestamps above 00:10. Remove them and retry.

Prop lock: the junk food is a rough-hewn wooden plate piled with plain burgers and fries. The apples sit in a wooden bowl on a flat rock beside the hearth. No brands, labels, or packaging. No dialogue in any shot.
