# Papa Yeti Hillside Roots 30s Panel Images and 3x2 Grids

Image prompts for `prompts/papa-yeti-hillside-roots-30s-storyboard.md`, which holds the video prompts. This file covers Video 6 only. Each 10-second video has, under its own heading, six panel image prompts, one per beat of its Agent prompt, timed and framed by the panel map in section 6 of `.agents/rules/cinematic-direction.md`. Each draws its panel's picture as a full single image with the full character lock of each Yeti in it from `.agents/rules/character-consistency.md`, the shot's light, and the panel's faces in its `Expressions:` sentence, as sections 2 to 4 of `.agents/rules/prompt-assembly.md` set out. Each video's 3x2 grid is composed from its six panel images by `compose_grid.ps1`; it has no prompt and is never drawn by an image model.

Images go in `3x2-timed-storyboard-images/papa-yeti-hillside-roots/`: each panel image as `Panel_<P>_V<N>.jpg` and each grid as `Grid_V<N>.jpg`. The panel images are vertical 9:16, and each grid is a 1920x1080 horizontal canvas.

To make a video's images:

1. Generate the six panel images one prompt at a time, each with the Yeti reference images of the Yetis in it attached, the prompt pasted whole, and the output set to vertical 9:16. Save each under the name its `Image:` line gives.
2. Check every panel image against its prompt and against the look check in section 7 of `.agents/rules/prompt-assembly.md`. Then check the six side by side for the same look across the set. Regenerate any image that fails.
3. Video 6: Papa Yeti is the only character, in panels 1 to 3; panels 4 to 6 have no character. Never attach `Start frame V6` to a panel image: Papa Yeti's chest and belly are pale in it. The Video 6 clip was refused three times for third-party content; keep every seedling small and still, every planting done by hand with the trowel, and no glowing, growing, sweeping light, or sunset in any panel. Never call Papa Yeti a monster or a creature.
4. Compose the grid: `pwsh -File .agents/skills/create-3x2-timed-image/compose_grid.ps1 -File prompts/papa-yeti-hillside-roots-30s-3x2.md -Video 6`.
5. If the image model refuses a prompt, the filter notes in the storyboard's Instruction 2 and section 10 of `prompt-assembly.md` apply. Change only that prompt's picture and effects text and keep every lock.
