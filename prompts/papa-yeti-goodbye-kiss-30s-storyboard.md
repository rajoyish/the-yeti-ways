# Papa Yeti Goodbye Kiss 30s Storyboard

On a golden mountain morning, Papa Yeti trudges away from the family cottage, tired and glum, with an empty woven basket in his hand, until Mama Yeti bursts out of the red door, runs down the flagstone path, and throws her arms around him. The basket tumbles onto the stones, his drooping face melts into a smile, they share a nose-rub kiss, and Babu Yeti hands back the fallen basket before Papa Yeti sets off again with a bounce in his step while Mama Yeti and Babu Yeti wave goodbye. The tone runs from wistful and weary to surprised and swept-up, then to warm and cheerful. It is cut shot by shot, one subject at a time, and has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines.

Everything below is written for the Google Flow Agent: three instruction cards for the project, one setup prompt, and one Agent prompt per video. The Image map and the `Images:` lines say which images go in the prompt box for each paste. The saved Characters @Papa Yeti, @Mama Yeti, and @Babu Yeti carry every Yeti's look, so nothing here describes them.

## Google Flow Agent instructions

### Instruction 1 - Yeti family series

Reference: None

```text
This project makes one original 3D animated short film about a family of three Yetis. Every video is made with Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 (Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, exactly 10 seconds long, with music and sound effects and no dialogue. Generate every video at 720p, never 360p, unless I ask for a 360p draft.

The Yetis are the saved Characters @Papa Yeti, @Mama Yeti, and @Babu Yeti. Use only the ones my prompt names, and draw each exactly as its saved Character. Never redesign, recolour, or restyle them, and never add another Yeti, a duplicate, or anyone my prompt does not name. Their sizes never change: whenever Babu Yeti stands with a grown-up, the top of his curl reaches only the grown-up's belly, and he is never placed nearer the camera than a grown-up beside him.

Every Yeti hand has five separate fingers, never three or four, and no claws. Every Yeti back is thick fur from shoulders to knees with no tail. Nothing is ever on a Yeti's head but its own tuft, and they wear nothing.

Style: stylized 3D CGI with ultra-realistic soft fur strands and cinematic light with a soft rim on the fur. No on-screen text, captions, logos, borders, or split screens.
```

### Instruction 2 - Goodbye Kiss story

Reference: `Plate - Meadow Cottage`

```text
Film: Goodbye Kiss, 30 seconds in three 10-second videos, wistful and weary, then swept-up, then warm and cheerful. Papa Yeti trudges away from home for the day, glum, holding an empty woven bamboo basket; Mama Yeti runs out and hugs him, the basket drops, his face melts into a smile, they share a nose-rub kiss, and Babu Yeti hands back the basket before Papa Yeti sets off happy.

Cast: only the three Yetis, no one else, ever. Papa Yeti in all videos; his change from weary to happy is the heart of the film. Mama Yeti in all videos. Babu Yeti in Video 3 only. No story cast.

Cutting: one subject at a time; the whole family shares a frame only in the last shot of Video 3.

Location: Plate - Meadow Cottage, used in all three videos. Video 1 opens on Plate - Meadow Cottage, Video 2 on End frame V1, Video 3 on End frame V2. No scene break.

Light: low golden morning sun from upper frame left as a backlight and rim on the fur, warm porch lanterns and amber windows at the cottage; soft and wistful, then bright and golden for the hug, then clear, warm morning.

Music: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, warm cello, and glockenspiel. Slow and sleepy guitar with low cello at first, glockenspiel enters when the door opens, the music quickens with Mama Yeti's run and swells into the hug, then settles to a gentle walking tempo and resolves on one long glockenspiel note. No on-screen text anywhere. If a video is refused, change "nose-rub kiss" to "nose rub" and "throws her arms around him" to "hugs him".
```
