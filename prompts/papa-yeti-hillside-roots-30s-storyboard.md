# Papa Yeti Hillside Roots 30s Storyboard

On a grey afternoon in a Himalayan hill village, Terrace Farmer's boot snaps an old boundary stake, the slope above his homestead cracks and slides over his empty hut, and as a second wave of earth rolls toward him, Papa Yeti leaps down between them and slams a fallen pine trunk down across the ground in front of him, and the earth piles up against it and stops short of the farmer. Then Papa Yeti plants a young alder sapling, the farmer and the village plant the bare slope so new roots will hold it, and Papa Yeti waves goodbye in the low gold sun. The tone runs from calm and pastoral to urgent and loud, then to grateful and warm. It is cut shot by shot, one subject at a time, and has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines.

Everything below is written for the Google Flow Agent: three instruction cards for the project, one setup prompt, and one Agent prompt per video. The Image map and the `Images:` lines say which images go in the prompt box for each paste. The saved Characters @Papa Yeti, @Terrace Farmer, and @Pied Goat carry every character's look, so nothing here describes them.
