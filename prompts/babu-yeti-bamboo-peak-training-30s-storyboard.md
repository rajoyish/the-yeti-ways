# Babu Yeti Bamboo Peak Training 30s Storyboard

On a golden evening high in the Himalaya, Babu Yeti races along a dusty trail, grabs three bamboo staffs, scrambles up a steep rock wall to the summit, balances on the tip of a swaying bamboo stalk, and spins a ring of spray from a stream stone that falls away to reveal the old mountain teacher waiting on a stone sparring platform. After a quick exchange of blocks, Babu Yeti leaps and presses his small hand against the teacher's raised palm, and the teacher answers with a small golden-yellow leaf. The film moves from bright and daring to tender and ends with a bow above a sea of golden mist. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines.
