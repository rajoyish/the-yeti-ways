# Yeti Family Everest Clean-Up 60s Storyboard

High on Mount Everest, the Yeti family discovers a massive pile of long-abandoned climber trash uncovered by melting glacial snow. When an exhausted team of Sherpa clean-up workers struggles under the weight of the frozen debris in the thin air, the Yetis use their legendary strength to carry the heaviest loads down to the lower camp, restoring their snowy mountain home to a sparkling, pristine sanctuary. The moral is that keeping nature clean is a shared responsibility, and true strength is using your gifts to help others protect our planet. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines. There is no dialogue; every line from the original story is carried by gesture, expression, and sound.

The Sherpa clean-up team is depicted in the same stylized 3D animated cartoon aesthetic as the Yetis, with rounded, friendly features and simple, textless mountaineering clothing, never photorealistic and never carrying brands or logos. The litter is depicted in a clean, family-friendly manner as weather-worn tent fabric, bent ladder frames, tarnished metal oxygen canisters, and burlap sacks, completely free of any biohazards or unsanitary elements. All mountain terrain is framed on broad, level snow cols and gentle slopes to avoid perilous heights or dangerous stunts.
