# Storyboard: The Gentle Fix

## 1. Goal and Audience
**Role & Platform:** Expert social media content creator specializing in highly engaging, compliant short-form video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels).
**Goal:** Create an 18-second heartwarming social media video showcasing the caring relationship between the Male Yeti and She-Yeti.
**Audience:** Global audience of all ages.

## 2. Safety Guardrails & Rules
* **No Dialogue:** The video relies strictly on background music and sound effects to communicate universally, excluding all spoken words.
* **Safety & Compliance:** Strictly follows all community guidelines: no harm, no dangerous activities, maintaining a safe, inclusive, and positive tone.
* **Character Consistency:** Uses exact, unmodified character descriptions and style locks to ensure consistency across all shots.

## 3. Format
The storyboard is formatted in a 4-column table, balancing timing, cinematography, precise generative AI prompts, and audio cues.
