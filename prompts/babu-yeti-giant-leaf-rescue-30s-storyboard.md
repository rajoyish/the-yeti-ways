# Babu Yeti Giant Leaf Rescue 30s Storyboard

High in the leafy canopy of the Himalayan foothills, a sleeping striped squirrel wakes in sudden startle and scurries toward the bamboo grove where Papa Yeti, Mama Yeti, and Babu Yeti are walking with a young apprentice red panda. The panicked squirrel leaps from a slanted bamboo stalk over a vast cliff edge and plummets into the foggy ravine, until Babu Yeti claps his paws with fierce determination to guide a giant mountain leaf caught in a rising updraft, swooping mid-air to catch the falling squirrel at the climax and carry it back to safety. The Yeti family and the red panda settle the exhausted squirrel into a mossy tree branch, watching over its peaceful slumber in the warm golden morning light. The tone moves from tranquil curiosity to breathless panic, through decisive heroism, and into tender, heartwarming relief. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines.

## Agent instructions

This storyboard is three 10-second Google Flow videos that cut together into one 30-second scene. The file holds the video prompts only: each video's `Character locks:` block and table, pasted together, are the text prompt for one Flow generation. The images each clip is generated with, the video's six panel images and the 3x2 grid composed from them, are listed in `prompts/babu-yeti-giant-leaf-rescue-30s-3x2.md` under the same `## Video N - <Title>` heading, with the steps for generating and checking them. No clip starts or ends on a supplied frame.
