# Babu Yeti and the Indigo Archer 60s Storyboard

Babu Yeti watches from a rain-sheltered village eave as Indigo Archer defends frightened villagers from a band of masked prowlers with breathtaking acrobatic defense and a precision bell-ricochet shot, teaching that courage and clever resourcefulness protect the community. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines. There is no dialogue; every line from the original story is carried by gesture, expression, and sound.

Every video is vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, 720p. The video workflow below is written for the Google Flow Agent: three instruction cards for the project, one setup prompt, and one Agent prompt per video. Two separate promotional cover prompts follow the final video. The Image map and the `Images:` lines say which images go in the prompt box for each paste. The saved Characters @Babu Yeti, @Indigo Archer, @Elder Villager, @Village Boy, and @Masked Prowler carry every character's look in video prompts. The cover stills repeat the full character locks for an independent image model. The file also holds 4 pick-up clips for the edit, each placed directly below the video it cuts into: Pick-up 4 below Video 2, Pick-up 3 below Video 3, Pick-ups 1 and 2 below Video 4.
