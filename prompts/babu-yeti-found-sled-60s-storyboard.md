# Babu Yeti and the Found Sled 60s Storyboard

Babu Yeti finds a bright red sled left behind on the mountain and wants to keep it. Mama and Papa Yeti gently show him why giving it back is the kind choice. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines. There is no dialogue; every line from the original story is carried by gesture, expression, and sound.

## Agent instructions

This storyboard is six 10-second Google Flow videos that cut together into one 60-second film. Follow these steps in order.

1. Set Flow's output format to vertical 9:16 before generating anything, and check it again before each clip. Flow falls back to 16:9 on a new session.
2. Generate Video 1 from its three shots as one 10-second clip. Paste the three table rows in order as a single prompt; do not generate them separately.
3. Check the clip is vertical 9:16, that no Yeti has anything on its head besides its tuft of fur, and that the final frame matches the `End frame:` line under the Video 1 table. If any of these is wrong, regenerate Video 1 before continuing.
4. Generate each following video by extending from the final frame of the one before it, using that video's table as the prompt. Repeat all three checks after every clip.
5. Video 5 starts a new scene on the lower trail. Do not extend it. Generate it fresh from its own prompt, matching the `Scene break:` line under the Video 4 table. Confirm the output format is still 9:16, because a fresh generation is where it most often reverts. Then extend Video 6 from the final frame of Video 5 as usual.
6. Cut the six clips together in order with no transitions. Keep the music continuous across the cuts.

Every video's table runs on its own 00:00 - 00:10 clock. Paste one table into Flow at a time, and paste only the table. The `Film position:` line under each heading is where the clip sits in the finished 60-second cut; it is for the editor, not for Flow. If Flow produces more than one clip, a clip longer than 10 seconds, or a timing error, the prompt contained timestamps above 00:10. Remove them and retry.

Every visual cell opens with `Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, full-frame vertical composition.` Keep that line in the prompt even when Flow's output format is already set to 9:16. The setting and the line together are what stop it reverting to widescreen. If a clip renders 16:9, regenerate it with the format reset rather than cropping, because cropping throws away the top and bottom of the framing.

Do not change the aspect ratio line, style lock, environment sentences, head lock, or character descriptions in any prompt. Only the `Action:` and audio text differ between shots. If Flow rejects a clip, the cause is almost always a brand, a real device, a named stock sound effect, on-screen text, or dialogue. Reword the action or audio, never the character text.

Aspect ratio lock: vertical 9:16 for every clip, set in Flow's output format and stated in the first line of every prompt.

Head lock: no Yeti has horns, antlers, spikes, or anything on its head besides its tuft of fur. Every prompt says so after the character text. If a clip comes back with anything on a Yeti's head, regenerate that clip before extending from it. A horned frame passed to the next clip carries the horns through the rest of the film. The most likely place for this to go wrong is Video 4, where the blue Yeti mimes sleep; the action keeps his paws below his chin for that reason, so do not reword it to bring the paws up beside his head.

Prop lock: one bright red sled, smooth and glossy with gently curved runners and a short loop of plain rope at the front, with no markings, stickers, or logos. In Videos 1 through 4 it is wedged in a low ice ledge and then held by Babu Yeti. In Video 5 he pulls it along the trail by its rope loop and sets it upright against the base of the wooden signpost, where it stays for Video 6. The signpost is carved wood with a plain arrow-shaped board and no writing. No other props. No dialogue in any shot.

Environment lock: Videos 1 through 4 use "Environment: A bright snowy slope high in the Whispering Mountains, with low ice ledges, softly drifting snowflakes, and distant blue-white peaks under a pale winter sky." Videos 5 and 6 use "Environment: A gentle lower mountain trail of packed snow winding between snow-dusted pine trees, with a tall carved wooden signpost topped by a plain arrow-shaped board with no writing standing beside the path, under warm late-afternoon light."

Position lock: Mama Yeti is always on the left, Babu Yeti in the center, Papa Yeti on the right. In two-shots the pair keeps that order (Mama left and Babu right, or Babu left and Papa right).

Music lock: one light, playful flute melody over soft strings runs through the whole minute. Only its state changes between shots.
