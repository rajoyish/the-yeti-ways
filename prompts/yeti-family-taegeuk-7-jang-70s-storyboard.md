# Yeti Family Taegeuk 7 Jang Storyboard

Papa Yeti, Mama Yeti, and Babu Yeti perform the complete, traditional Taekwondo Taegeuk 7 Jang (Chil Jang) poomsae upon an ancient Himalayan mountain sanctuary terrace, embodying the martial essence of the Mountain—immovable balance, centered strength, and harmonious family unity. Witnessed and honored by two wise Himalayan red pandas seated upon the perimeter stone pedestals, the Yeti family executes every authentic stance, block, and strike in unison: from the opening Charyeot attention stance, Kyeong-rae bow, and Jumbi ready stance, through the intricate tiger stances, scissors blocks, knee strikes, cross stance uppercuts, crescent kicks, and target elbow strikes, culminating in a thunderous synchronized Kihap side punch and a serene closing bow, followed in Videos 8 and 9 by a spirited, respectful martial arts spar between Papa Yeti and the Elder Himalayan Red Panda. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, cinematic direction, and social media prompt guidelines. There is no dialogue; every posture and emotion is carried by deliberate martial movement, disciplined breathing, and the soulful melody of a solo Daegeum bamboo flute.
