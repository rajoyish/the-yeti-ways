# Papa Yeti Five Stance Kung Fu 30s Storyboard

This storyboard portrays Papa Yeti performing the Five Stance form (Wu Bu Quan) on a snowy stone terrace while Babu Yeti watches and copies each stance a beat behind him, wobbling and plopping but never giving up, until the two finish with a shared salute. It follows the man's sequence exactly: Horse Stance, Bow Stance, Resting Stance, Empty Stance, Single-Leg Stance, and Drop Stance, mirrored to the left, then the closing salute. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines.

## Agent instructions

This storyboard is three 10-second Google Flow videos that cut together into one 30-second scene. Follow these steps in order.

1. Generate Video 1 from its three shots as one 10-second clip. Paste the three table rows in order as a single prompt; do not generate them separately.
2. Take the final frame of Video 1. Confirm it matches the `End frame:` line under the Video 1 table. If it does not, regenerate Video 1 before continuing.
3. Generate Video 2 by extending from that final frame, using the Video 2 table as the prompt. Repeat the end-frame check.
4. Generate Video 3 by extending from the final frame of Video 2.
5. Cut the three clips together in order with no transitions. Keep the audio continuous across the cuts.

Every video's table runs on its own 00:00 - 00:10 clock. Paste one table into Flow at a time, and paste only the table. The `Film position:` line under each heading is where the clip sits in the finished 30-second cut; it is for the editor, not for Flow. If Flow produces more than one clip, a clip longer than 10 seconds, or a timing error, the prompt contained timestamps above 00:10. Remove them and retry.

Do not change the style lock, environment sentence, or character descriptions in any prompt. Only the `Action:` and audio text differ between shots. If Flow rejects a clip, the cause is almost always a brand, a real device, a named stock sound effect, or dialogue. Reword the action or audio, never the character text.

Prop lock: no props and no clothing. The stances are practised solo in open air with no partner, no target, and no contact. The only set dressing is the flat stone terrace, the cave mouth behind it, and the snowy valley beyond. No brands, labels, or packaging. No dialogue in any shot.
