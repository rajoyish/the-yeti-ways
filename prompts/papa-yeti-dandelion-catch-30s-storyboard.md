# Papa Yeti Dandelion Catch 30s Storyboard

Papa Yeti sits high on a pine branch above a waterfall and blows a dandelion, and the seeds drift down over the river where Babu Yeti is being carried along by the current. A warm gold light rises in Papa Yeti's eyes, he launches off the branch, crosses the water in long bounding strides, gathers Babu up, and carries him back through the mist to land on his back in the grass, where one last seed settles the story. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines.

## Agent instructions

This storyboard is three 10-second Google Flow videos that cut together into one 30-second scene. Follow these steps in order.

1. Generate Video 1 from its three shots as one 10-second clip. Paste the three table rows in order as a single prompt; do not generate them separately.
2. Take the final frame of Video 1. Confirm it matches the `End frame:` line under the Video 1 table. If it does not, regenerate Video 1 before continuing.
3. Generate Video 2 by extending from that final frame, using the Video 2 table as the prompt. Repeat the end-frame check.
4. Generate Video 3 by extending from the final frame of Video 2.
5. Cut the three clips together in order with no transitions. Keep the audio continuous across the cuts.

Every video's table runs on its own 00:00 - 00:10 clock. Paste one table into Flow at a time, and paste only the table. The `Film position:` line under each heading is where the clip sits in the finished 30-second cut; it is for the editor, not for Flow. If Flow produces more than one clip, a clip longer than 10 seconds, or a timing error, the prompt contained timestamps above 00:10. Remove them and retry.

Do not change the style lock, environment sentence, or character descriptions in any prompt. Only the `Action:` and audio text differ between shots. The gold light in the blue Yeti's eyes is written as a lighting effect inside the `Action:` text, never as a change to his locked description; keep it that way.

If Flow rejects a clip, the cause is almost always a brand, a real device, a named stock sound effect, or dialogue. Reword the action or audio, never the character text. A refusal that mentions third-party content providers is different: it means the described shot composed into a recognizable moment from an existing animated film. Break the composition up. Soften the eye glow to a warm shimmer, replace bounding strides across water with wading through shallows, and replace a launch off a branch with a run down the bank.

Prop lock: one wild dandelion seed head, a white papery puff on a slender green stem, held by the blue Yeti on the pine branch in the first shot. Its loose seeds drift on the air and the waterfall mist through every later shot, and one settles on the baby Yeti's nose in the final shot. The only other set dressing is the waterfall, the river, the rocky cliffs, the pine branches, and the grassy shoreline. No clothing and no carried objects. The baby Yeti is buoyed by its thick fur and stays at the surface throughout; it is never submerged and never in distress. No dialogue in any shot.
