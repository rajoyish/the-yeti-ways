# Yeti Food Photography Storyboard

This storyboard humorously portrays a relatable modern dinner moment where Mama Yeti stops Papa Yeti from eating to take a picture of the food. It has been refined for direct AI video generation, complying with character design consistency and social media prompt standards.

Prop and audio note: the camera is an in-world carved ice tablet rather than a branded smartphone, and every sound cue is described from scratch instead of naming a known effect. Naming real devices or stock effects such as a record scratch trips Flow's third-party content filter.
