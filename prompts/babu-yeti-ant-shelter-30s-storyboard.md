# Babu Yeti Ant Shelter 30s Storyboard

Babu Yeti is watching a line of ants cross the moss in a sunlit forest clearing when the first heavy raindrop bursts beside their nest. As the storm breaks, he builds a peaked roof of fallen leaves over the trail, holds it up through the worst of the downpour, and wedges a twig under it so the ants can march home dry. The film ends with Babu throwing his arms wide in the rain, then sitting by his shelter as the sun comes back. The tone is small, tense, and warm, shot almost entirely in cinematic close-ups on Babu's face and the ants' level. It has been strictly formatted for AI video generation according to character styling rules, environment locks, and social media prompt guidelines.
