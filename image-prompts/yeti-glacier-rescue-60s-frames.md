# Yeti Glacier Rescue 60s Reference Stills

Reference still prompts for `prompts/yeti-glacier-rescue-60s-storyboard.md`. Three stills per 10-second video, one per shot, eighteen in total, numbered in film order. Each still shows the picture its shot closes on (the shot's third beat, at 00:03, 00:07, or 00:10 on the video's clock), the same picture as panel 3, 6, or 9 of the video's 3x3 grid in `prompts/yeti-glacier-rescue-60s-3x3.md`. Each prompt opens with `Create image:` and carries the same aspect ratio line, style lock, environment sentence, lighting sentence, character text with its `[EXPRESSION]` slot, head lock, look lock, scale lock (where the shot has one), `Animals:` sentence, and avoid line as its shot, with the `Action:` rewritten as a single held image framed as the shot ends, and the shot's effects frozen in an `Effects:` sentence.

These stills are for the stills route. In Google Flow, generate a video in ingredients-to-video mode with its three stills attached as reference images and its storyboard table pasted as the prompt. Never set a still as a start or an end frame.

Attach the family reference image to every still generation as a character reference, and run the look check in section 12 of `.agents/rules/character-consistency.md` on every still before using it: each Yeti's colour, tuft, and size, nothing worn, no extra Yeti, and the face the prompt names. A still that fails is regenerated, because the clip copies its look from the images attached to it.

This film is widescreen, so every prompt opens with the storyboard's 16:9 line rather than the vertical one. The adult fox appears in no still before Still 16, and the kit is wrapped in the grey blanket in every still before Still 17. Still 8 shows only the pink Yeti's arm; if the image model draws her whole body or her face, regenerate it. Stills 2, 5, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15, 16, and 18 have the baby Yeti and a grown-up Yeti in the same shot, so they carry the scale lock; in each of them the baby Yeti stands about half as tall as the grown-ups. Stills 1, 3, 4, and 17 show the baby Yeti with no grown-up Yeti, and if one appears in them, regenerate it. These stills replace the thirty start, end, and check frames this file held before the film moved to 3x3 grids; the fifteen frames that fell on the end of a shot carried over as Stills 1 to 3 and 7 to 18, and Stills 4 to 6 are new, following the prop lock's Video 2, in which the blue Yeti carries the snowball onto the pillar.
