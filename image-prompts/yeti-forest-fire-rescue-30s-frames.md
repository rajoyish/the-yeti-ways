# Yeti Forest Fire Rescue 30s Image Prompts

Still-frame image prompts for `prompts/yeti-forest-fire-rescue-30s-storyboard.md`. Five frames per video, fifteen in total, numbered in film order, at 00:00, 00:03, 00:05, 00:07, and 00:10 on each video's own clock. The 00:00 and 00:10 frames of each video are the start image and the end image the video is generated from in Google Flow; the 00:03, 00:05, and 00:07 frames are what the clip is checked against. Each prompt opens with `Create image:` and carries the same aspect ratio line, style lock, environment sentence, character text, head lock, and `Animals:` sentence as the shot its instant belongs to, with the `Action:` rewritten as a single held image at that instant. The `[EXPRESSION]` slot in each character text is filled per frame from the table in section 5 of `.agents/rules/character-consistency.md`, matching the feeling the Yeti holds at that instant. The 00:00 frame of each video after the first is the handoff from the previous video's end image, seen through the new video's opening shot.

Frame 8 is the Climax peak (film 00:15) and is the only frame where all three Yetis strain. Frame 3 is the only frame with the doe running. The doe is absent from Frames 4 to 11 and comes back in Frame 12; every prompt that omits her says so, because an image model draws every character it is given unless told otherwise. No prompt carries audio, music, or a timestamp.
