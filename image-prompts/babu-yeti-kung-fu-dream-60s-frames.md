# Babu Yeti's Kung Fu Dream 60s Image Prompts

Still-frame image prompts for `prompts/babu-yeti-kung-fu-dream-60s-storyboard.md`. One frame per shot, eighteen in total, numbered in film order. Each prompt carries the same aspect ratio line, style lock, environment sentence, character text, head lock, and `Creatures:` sentence as the matching video shot, with the `Action:` rewritten as a single held image. The `[EXPRESSION]` slot in each character text is filled per frame from the table in section 5 of `.agents/rules/character-consistency.md`, matching the feeling the Yeti holds in that frame.

Frames 3, 6, 9, 12, and 15 are the `End frame:` images under Videos 1 to 5, the frames Flow freezes and extends from. Frames 4 and 16 are the opening images of the two fresh clips, Video 2 and Video 6, and can be handed to Flow as start images. Frame 18 is the closing image of the film.
