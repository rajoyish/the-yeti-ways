Flow Account Seeder

Characters

Works from any Flow page. Packs each Character's Portrait, Body and info, at full resolution, into one ZIP in Downloads/flow-seed-export/. Import that ZIP straight into the other account.

Plates

Open a film's project. Packs its Plate - … images into one ZIP in Downloads/flow-seed-export/.

To keep a copy in your library too: node tools/flow-account-seeder/seed.mjs collect (reads every ZIP there).

Seed folder

An export ZIP from the other account, or a seed from node tools/flow-account-seeder/seed.mjs build prompts/<film>-storyboard.md (or build --all): references/flow-seed/<name>.zip. It is unpacked here, in memory, as it is read.
