# Voices (Google Flow and AI Studio)

These are the 30 voices in Google Flow's Voices panel (screenshots of 2026-11-14), described as Flow describes them. Every one of them is also a Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS voice in Google AI Studio, under the same name. Only these voices are used, for every speaker, so a character's saved Flow Character speaks with the same voice as its recorded lines, and its lips move the way that voice speaks. AI Studio has 40 more voices (Riko, Zeno, Cruz, Lumi, and others) that Flow does not have; never use them.

Use the name exactly as written.

## Fixed voices

| Speaker | Voice | Flow description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrator | Charon | Male, informative, lower pitch |
| Papa Yeti | Iapetus | Male, clear, mid-low pitch |
| Mama Yeti | Erinome | Female, clear, mid pitch |
| Babu Yeti | Puck | Male, upbeat, mid pitch (styled as a small young boy) |

The Narrator's voice may be changed for a story (keep it one voice for the whole story); the three Yeti voices never change. Never give a story character a fixed voice, and never give two speakers the same voice.
