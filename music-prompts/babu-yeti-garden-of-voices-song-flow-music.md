# Many Voices, One Song - Google Flow Music

The closing song of **Babu Yeti and the Garden of Voices**, from the `## Song` section of [stories/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-story.md](../stories/babu-yeti-garden-of-voices-story.md). No storyboard exists yet (the film is audio-first), so the song is made on its own and placed into the narration after "From that day on, Babu Yeti gave up his pride forever.", before the Narrator's moral, before the film's timeline is built.

- **Song:** about 1:50. A two-bar flute intro (no singing), five 4-line verses at two bars a line, and one held closing chord. Steady 4/4 at 96 BPM (one bar = 2.5 s, 40 sung bars = 100 s), G major.
- **Voices:** Verse 1 is Babu Yeti alone, a small, bright, young boy's voice. Verses 2, 3 and 5 are everyone together, a warm children's-and-family choir in unison. Verse 4 is the choir with a warm, gentle woman's voice (Mama Yeti) leading on top.
- **Instruments:** a bamboo flute (Babu's trill), a madal and soft hand drums (the mice's feet), a low cello hum (Gombu Tortoise), a light whistle-flute (Mili Red Panda), and a wooden guiro chirrup (Nimbu Tree Frog).

Flow Music invents its own song ("Counting Every Breath" and the like) when a prompt carries timestamps or a script, so the prompts below hold only musical terms and the verbatim lyrics. The lyrics are original, so the famous-rhyme block seen with Humpty Dumpty does not apply.

## Procedure

**Method B, Compose (recommended, and what the agent drives):** open a new session at `https://www.flowmusic.app/`, open **Compose**, switch **Instrumental** off, paste the Lyrics Box, paste the Sound Box, switch **Advanced** on and set **BPM 96**, **Length 1:50**, **Model Lyria 3.5**, title `Many Voices, One Song`, then **Generate**. If it asks "Which do you prefer?", listen to both and choose; keep both. Download the chosen song as **WAV** (More → Download → WAV).

**Method A, Direct Chat:** paste Prompt 1 into a new session and send.

## Prompt 1 - Direct Chat

```text
Make one complete song, about 1 minute 50 seconds long, titled "Many Voices, One Song": a gentle, joyful children's sing-along in G major, steady 4/4 at 96 BPM. Instruments: a bright bamboo flute playing a sweet high trill, a madal and soft hand drums keeping a light walking beat, a low warm cello hum underneath, a light whistle-flute, and a wooden guiro chirrup. Start with two bars of flute alone, no singing. Verse 1 is sung by one young boy alone, small, bright and high. Verses 2, 3 and 5 are sung by a warm children's and family choir in unison. In Verse 4 a warm, gentle woman's voice leads on top of the choir. Strictly no adult male lead voice and no baritone. End on one warm held chord, with no outro.
Sing ONLY these exact supplied lyrics word-for-word. DO NOT invent, rewrite, summarize, or substitute lyrics. DO NOT add an [Intro] vocalise (e.g. "mmm", "la la la"), [Chorus], [Bridge], or [Outro]. Follow the exact section order: [Verse 1] through [Verse 5].

[Verse 1]
I used to think my song was best,
louder and sweeter than the rest,
but one small voice, all on its own,
sounds lonely singing all alone.

[Verse 2]
Hum, hum, goes Gombu, deep and slow,
Mili whistles soft and low,
tap-tap-tap, the mice keep time,
and Nimbu chirrups every line.

[Verse 3]
Many flowers make a garden bright,
red and yellow, blue and white,
many voices, big and small,
make the sweetest song of all.

[Verse 4]
Sing your song and I'll sing mine,
side by side our voices shine,
Mama hums and Babu trills,
over the glade and over the hills.

[Verse 5]
Many flowers make a garden bright,
red and yellow, blue and white,
many voices, big and small,
make the sweetest song of all.
```

## In-app correction prompts

- **Wrong words or a different song:** `Remake this song singing ONLY the exact lyrics I supplied, word for word, in the order [Verse 1] to [Verse 5]. Do not invent, change, or add any lyrics.`
- **Unwanted intro singing, chorus, or outro:** `Remove the sung intro, any chorus, bridge, or outro. Keep two bars of flute alone at the start, then the five verses, then one held chord.`
- **Adult male voice:** `Keep the same song, but Verse 1 must be one young boy's small, bright, high voice, and the other verses a children's and family choir. No adult male lead voice.`
- **Too fast or too slow:** `Keep the song and the lyrics, at a steady 96 BPM, each lyric line two bars long, about 1 minute 50 seconds in all.`

## Checking the download

Transcribe the WAV with whisper.cpp (`/opt/homebrew/bin/whisper-cli -m ~/.cache/whisper-cpp/ggml-medium.en.bin`) and compare it with the Lyrics Box: sung words come back less cleanly than speech, so read every difference against the audio. `ffprobe` gives the length.
